These videos demonstrate the process for posting audio recordings of sermons on the church website.
There are four basic steps in this process.
1. Get information about the sermon, such as the title, speaker, and relevant scripture.
2. Use the live stream video on YouTube to extract the audio from the service.
3. Edit the audio to only include the sermon and make adjustments to the audio if needed.
4. Post the audio to the website, optionally adding any relevant scripture to the post.
1. Get information about the sermon.
The main church website, provided by Squarespace, has a hidden form that can be used by clergy to specify details about a sermon, including the title, speaker, and optional relevant scripture.
This form can be reached by using the web page address shown, www.al saints woodbridge.org slash sermon dash form.
Let's take a look at how this form is set up with Squarespace and how to make changes to the form.
To access the Squarespace website configuration pages, enter config after the church website address as shown.
Then log in with your email address and password.
After logging in under website, select the pages link.
The sermon form is listed under the not linked section, and at the time of this recording, near the bottom of the list.
Scroll down to find the form.
Click on the form name to view the contents.
For an example of making changes to the form, we will add a new option to the form's speaker selection.
To change the form, move the mouse over the form, and then select edit from the menu that appears near the top of the form.
Next, click on the form in the area where the data entry fields are shown. Then select the pencil icon to make changes.
Click on edit form fields.
Then select the field to change. For now, we want to change speaker.
Scroll down to the end to add a new option.
Click add option and then type the text for the new option.
Then drag and drop the option into the desired position within the list.
Click back to stop editing the field. Then back again to stop editing the form fields.
Next we scroll down to verify the option has been added to speaker.
Click save near the top to save the changes to the form.
Next, let's look at how to configure what happens when a form is submitted.
How do we view the information that has been entered on the form?
Let's edit the form again by hovering the mouse over the form.
Select edit.
Then click on the form and select the pencil icon.
Then select storage at the top.
Under email notification, it shows the email address that will receive the data from any forms that are submitted.
The address can be changed by clicking on it and entering a new address.
For now, we will leave the address as is.
Click back and then cancel to exit the form.
If you are tasked with posting sermon recordings to the website, usually you will not be using these forms to enter data,
and typically the format of the form does not often change.
Most of the time, you will just need to look at the submitted form data that is sent to the configured email address.
To get the information submitted on a sermon form, log in to Google Mail using the ASC sermon forms at gmail.com address.
Here we can see a recently submitted form has been sent and appears as a new email message.
In the contents of the email message are the responses entered onto the form for a particular sermon.
